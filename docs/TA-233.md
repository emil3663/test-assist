### TA-233 — The capture cursor/reticle does not track the region actually being drawn

- **Phase:** 3
- **Priority:** P3
- **Suggested labels:** `bug`, `capture`, `ux`, `multi-display`
- **Problem it solves:** During today's Block A pass (DSP-03, laptop @125%), the
  reporter marked up a quick-capture screenshot with two arrows: one pointing at
  where the capture target/reticle appeared to be, the other at where the
  highlighted selection rectangle was actually being drawn — the two do not
  coincide (`capture frame on laptop visible for quick cqpture.png`). The
  region capture itself still produced correct content (`region capture on
  laptop.png` reads `4LAPTOP 5LAPTOP 6LAPTOP 7LA...`, confirming the export is
  right), so this is specifically about the on-screen feedback during the drag
  being misleading, not about what gets exported.

  **Not yet measured.** The evidence is a hand-annotated screenshot (arrows
  added by the reporter), not raw coordinates, so the offset's direction and
  magnitude have not been pinned down the way TA-232's brightness-step
  boundary was. Two different mechanisms could produce this symptom and
  haven't been distinguished:
  - a genuinely separate rendering bug (the drawn rectangle lags or offsets
    from the tracked cursor position), or
  - the same coordinate discontinuity TA-223 hypothesizes at the mixed-DPI
    screen boundary, visible here as a static mismatch rather than a live
    jump.
- **Scope:**
  - Reproduce on the reference rig with the same coordinate logging TA-223's
    scope calls for (`event.globalPosition()` vs `QScreen.geometry()` during
    the drag), and check whether the reticle/cursor indicator and the drawn
    rectangle are computed from the same coordinate source.
  - If it shares TA-223's mechanism, fold this into that ticket rather than
    fixing twice; if it's a distinct rendering path (e.g., the reticle is
    drawn from raw device coordinates while the rectangle uses logical ones,
    or vice versa), fix independently.
- **Deliverables:** A confirmed measurement of what the reticle position and
  the rectangle bounds actually are at a few sample cursor positions,
  including at least one near the laptop/external boundary; a fix or a
  documented merge into TA-223.
- **Acceptance criteria:** The visible target indicator and the edge of the
  highlighted selection agree, at rest and while dragging, on both screens.
- **Dependencies:** Possibly the same root cause as **TA-223** — confirm with
  coordinate logging before treating as separate. Do not fix twice.
